### 1.	Module aims and learning outcomes
#### 1.1 Introduction
This module aims to provide students with the ability to study and reflect on key principles and methods in research based on the scientific method in the Computing subject areas, developing research competencies, data analysis skills, a critical design and independent learning. The module covers various tools and techniques that you, as a researcher, may need and allows for feedback throughout the module through cases and project tasks. The module also provides essential insight into the legal, social, ethical and professional issues applicable to computing projects, as well as the relevant codes of practice and practices. The two core textbooks offer a comprehensive overview of the subject, based around the scientific method and offering details for doing research in various types of organisations. You will be required to identify with research practicalities, as well as use critical analysis of the key principles of research methodology to bring together the knowledge gained throughout other modules on your programme. There will be continuous assessment throughout with case studies and milestone assessments. Hence facilitating peer-to-peer, as well as instructor interactions. You will be able to demonstrate your ability and strengths through evidence and reflections by maintaining an e-Portfolio for this module. This module's assessment has been explicitly designed to allow you to work on your project's proposal. This module ends at the moment you will start of the Project Module where you will be able to work with your allocated supervisor on the output of this Research Methods, Ethics application included.

#### 1.2 A summary of the learning outcomes of the module
•	Acquire the ability to study and reflect on key principles and methods in research based on the scientific method and relevant to various disciplines.

•	Acquire the ability to examine various research strategies and designs as applicable to projects at hand.

•	Acquire the ability to develop research competencies, in particular those relating to the collection and analysis of data types to enable a critical design and evaluation of independent research.

•	Have the opportunity to take a reflective and independent approach to the learning process.

#### 1.3 Expected learning outcomes
•	Appraise the professional, legal, social, cultural and ethical issues that affect computing professionals.

•	Appraise the principles of academic investigation, applying them to a research topic in the applicable computing field.

•	Evaluate critically existing literature, research design and methodology for the chosen topic, including data analysis processes.

•	Produce and critically evaluate a research proposal for the chosen topic.
